Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website or engage our services, we may collect the following information:

  • Contact information: name, email address, and any details you provide through our contact form
  • Project information: business requirements, technical specifications, and materials shared during project engagements
  • Usage data: pages visited, time spent on site, browser type, approximate location (country/region), and device information collected automatically through analytics cookies only after you consent

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and contact form submissions
  • Deliver and manage our software development and consulting services
  • Communicate project updates, timelines, and deliverables
  • Improve our website experience and service offerings
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following cases:

  • Service providers: trusted third-party tools we use to operate our business (e.g., hosting, email, project management) under strict confidentiality agreements
  • Legal requirements: when required by law, court order, or to protect our legal rights
  • With your consent: when you have given us explicit permission to share specific information

4. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. This includes encrypted communications, secure hosting infrastructure, and access controls limiting data to authorized personnel only.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law. Contact form submissions are retained for up to 12 months. Project-related data is retained for the duration of our business relationship plus a reasonable period for record-keeping.

6. Cookies and Tracking

Essential cookies. We use strictly necessary cookies that keep the site secure and working (for example, the security token that protects our contact form). These are always active and cannot be turned off.

Analytics cookies. With your consent, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people visit our site, which pages they view, and whether they are new or returning visitors. These cookies set a randomized identifier; we enable IP anonymization so your full IP address is not stored by Google. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Your choice. When you first visit, a banner lets you Accept or Reject analytics cookies, and no analytics cookies are set unless you accept. You can change your decision at any time using the Cookie Settings link in the footer, or by clearing cookies in your browser. You can also install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and applicable Puerto Rico and U.S. privacy laws, including the right to:

  • Know and request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information (note: we do not sell or share your personal information)
  • Withdraw consent for analytics processing at any time
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us through our contact form. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third-party websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected data from a minor, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

11. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our contact form.
